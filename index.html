About Me

I’ve spent nearly two decades helping organizations of all sizes craft compelling narratives to secure the funding they need. Writing has been a constant throughout my life, beginning with childhood penpals, growing to encompass adolescent fiction and college literature papers, and more recent transformative opportunities like a prison poetry class. My fundraising perspective is informed by training in racial equity and asset-based community development and I am especially eager to support teams that are committed to anti-racism, that understand and address the systemic roots of inequalities, and that center the agency and dignity of their program beneficiaries. Given my privileges as a cis white woman, I am committed to my own ongoing learning and un-learning about racism, ableism, capitalism, colonialism, and other oppressive systems.

I live and work in central North Carolina from the unceded land of the Eno, Tutelo, Saponi, Occaneechi, and Shakori Native people. I acknowledge the ancestral & present land stewardship and place-based knowledge of the peoples of these territories.
